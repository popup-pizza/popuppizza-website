Cost a menu item from what you actually pay suppliers, then price it against a target food cost. Enter each ingredient once with its purchase price and pack size, and the tool works out the cost of the amount you actually use.

1. Ingredient Costs

Enter what you pay and the size you buy. For example, mozzarella at $12.80 for 1 kg. The tool converts this to a unit cost so recipes stay accurate when supplier prices change, update it here once and every item reprices.

2. Build the Item

Add each ingredient and how much goes on one item. Quantities are in the base unit, grams for anything bought by weight, millilitres by volume, or units for anything bought individually.
