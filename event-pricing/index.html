Event Basics

Guest count and appetite drive almost everything downstream.

On pizzas per head: around 1 works for a seated meal with sides, 1.5 or more if pizza is the only food and people are grazing over hours. Under-catering an event is far more damaging than a little waste.

Wastage is not optional. Dough is committed 72 hours ahead, so an over-estimate can't be unwound. Anything under about 5% is optimistic for a catering job.

Site & Venue

Some venues charge a flat fee, some take a cut of takings, some do both.

Staff

Count every paid hour, not just the hours you're serving.

Two things that get missed here. Prep hours cover the dough made three days ahead plus shopping and loading, that's real paid time even though it happens off-site. And penalty loading applies to weekends and public holidays, set it from your current award rate rather than guessing, since it materially changes the number.

Travel & Logistics

Running the vehicle and the oven.

Equipment wear is a per-event set-aside toward replacing the oven, peels, tables and gazebo. Easy to leave out, and leaving it out quietly funds your own depreciation.

Pricing

Set your target and the tool solves for the price. Or enter a price to see what margin it actually returns.

Margin here is on the whole job, after food, staff, site and travel. It is not food-cost margin, which is normally much higher, so don't compare the two directly.
