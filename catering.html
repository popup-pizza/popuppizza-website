Our Story

Small Batch, Cooked Properly

Pop-Up Pizza started with a simple idea: bring genuinely good, stone-baked pizza to people wherever they happen to be, rather than asking them to come to us.

Brett and Fiona built Pop-Up Pizza from the ground up, a gas-fired stone oven, a dough recipe fermented for 72 hours at 8°C, and a belief that catering doesn't need to mean compromise. Every event we cater gets the same base, the same care, and the same attention to detail as the pizzas we sell from our stall.

We started out at markets around Canberra, cooking one pizza at a time in front of people who'd never seen a stone oven up close. That's still the heart of what we do at events, pizza made fresh, in front of your guests, not reheated or trucked in from somewhere else.

As we've grown, so has what we bring to the table, literally. Bacciata, grazing boards and salads now sit alongside the pizza, each one built with the same ingredients and the same standards, so a full spread feels as considered as a single pizza off the stall.

When you book us for an event, you're getting the same two people who started this, still hands-on, still cooking, still designing every menu around the people eating it.
